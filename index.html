Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes, free to download and play, with no in-app purchases. The terms have the details.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The puzzles and stories are on your phone, so you can play without a connection, in the Android app and in the iPhone Home Screen app.

What happens to my data?

Your progress stays on your phone. The privacy policy says exactly what we collect, and you can delete your data at any time.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes, free, as a web app: open orangepulplabs.com/zest/iphone on your iPhone and add it to your Home Screen. It runs full screen and offline.

Who is it for?

Anyone aged 13 and up who likes a small puzzle and a good story.

Where do the stories come from?

Each story comes with its sources. Spot a mistake? Tap Report a mistake in the app and we will fix it, or contact us.
