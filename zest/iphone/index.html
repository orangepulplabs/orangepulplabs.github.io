For iPhone and iPad

Put Zest on your iPhone’s Home Screen

Zest on iPhone runs from Safari. Add it to your Home Screen once, and after that it opens like any other app, works offline, and has the same daily puzzle and all 39 puzzles.

  3. 3

Good to know

What to expect on iPhone

  • Works offlineAfter you open it once, everything is saved on your phone.
  • No accountYou don’t sign up for anything.
  • Optional daily remindersIn the Home Screen app, on iOS 16.4 or later. Turn them on or off in Settings.
  • Progress stays on your phoneSafari and the Home Screen app keep separate progress. To move it, open Settings, then Copy my progress.

Sign-in and rankings are only in the Android app for now. On Android? Get it on Google Play.
