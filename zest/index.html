Questions

Good to know

Is Zest free?

Yes, Zest is free to download and play. The terms have the details.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The puzzles and stories are on your phone, so you can play without a connection. No account is needed.

What happens to my data?

Your progress stays on your phone. The privacy policy says exactly what Zest collects, and you can delete your data at any time.

Is Zest on iPhone?

Not yet. An iPhone version is planned, so Zest is coming soon to the App Store.

Who is it for?

Anyone aged 13 and up who likes a small puzzle and a good story.

Where do the stories come from?

Each story comes with its sources. Spot a mistake? Tap Report a mistake in the app and we will fix it, or contact us.
