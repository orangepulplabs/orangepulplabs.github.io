Questions

Before you play

Is it free?

Yes. There’s nothing to buy inside the game. The terms have the details.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The puzzles and stories are saved on your phone, so Zest works without a connection, on Android and on an iPhone Home Screen.

What happens to my data?

Your progress is saved on your phone, not in an account. The privacy policy lists exactly what we collect, and you can delete your data at any time.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes. Open orangepulplabs.com/zest/iphone on your iPhone and add Zest to your Home Screen. It’s free, opens full screen and works offline.

Who is it for?

Anyone 13 or older who likes a puzzle and wants to know where it came from.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing is lost. You can go back and play the puzzles you missed. You can also set a weekly goal of 3, 4, 5 or 7 days, so you don’t have to play every day.

Where do the stories come from?

From books, magazines, patents and old puzzle collections. Every story lists its sources. If you spot a mistake, tap Report a mistake in the app or contact us, and we’ll check it.
