Zest privacy policy

Applies to the Zest Android app, the iPhone Home Screen app and this website · Effective 10 November 2026 · Published by OrangePulp Labs

Zest keeps your progress on your device. If you turn on reminders in the iPhone Home Screen app, or choose to sign in or join the rankings, a small amount of data goes to our service. This page says exactly what, and how to delete it.

1. Who we are

Zest is published by OrangePulp Labs, New Delhi, India. We decide what personal data Zest uses and why. Contact, questions and complaints: orangepulplabs@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

2. What stays on your device

Your plays, progress and settings are kept on your phone, or in your browser’s storage when you play on the web. Nothing else leaves your device unless you use one of the features below. If you tap Report a mistake on a story, Zest sends which story it is and the reason you picked, nothing about you. The friend challenge page on this website asks our service for the day’s puzzle when it opens. That request contains nothing about you.

2a. Anonymous usage counts

Once a day, if you are online, Zest sends a short list of totals such as “rounds played: 12” or “puzzles solved: 3”. These are counts of a fixed set of events, nothing more. They carry no account, no user ID, no device ID and no scores, and we do not store your IP address with them. We add them to a daily total across all players, which helps us see which parts of Zest are used and where people stop. You can switch this off in Settings under Share anonymous usage counts. Like any internet request, the request itself reaches our service provider (Cloudflare), which may see your IP address in transit.

2b. The web version (iPhone, iPad and browsers)

You can also play Zest in a web browser or from your iPhone’s Home Screen at orangepulplabs.com/zest/app/. It is the same game as the Android app and follows this same policy. Your progress is kept in your browser’s storage on your device, not on our servers. Safari and the Home Screen keep separate copies; Copy my progress in Settings puts your progress on your own clipboard as a code, and nothing is sent to us. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and like any website host, GitHub may see your IP address when your browser loads the pages. Sign-in and rankings are not in the web version yet.

2c. Reminders in the iPhone Home Screen app

Reminders on iPhone work only once Zest is added to your Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or later), and only if you allow them. When you do, two things go to our service on Cloudflare: your browser’s push address (a random web address made by Apple, Google or Mozilla so a notification can reach this browser) and the times of your next reminders. We get no name, no account and no reminder text: the words of each reminder stay on your device, and our service only sends an empty signal at each time. Each time is deleted once it is sent. The push address and all times are deleted when you turn reminders off, and after 30 days without an update. To stop reminders, turn them off in Zest’s Settings, or in iPhone Settings, Notifications, Zest. Removing Zest from your Home Screen also stops them.

3. If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and for adults aged 18 and over. It lets you use the same progress on your phone and on the website. It is also required to join the rankings.

  • What we ask Google for: only a sign-in confirmation and your Google user ID (the openid permission). We do not ask Google for your age, date of birth, contacts or anything else. While it checks the sign-in, Google may tell our service your email address. We do not store it.
  • Your age group is the one you choose in Zest, for example “25 to 34”. We keep that group, not a date of birth. It keeps sign-in and rankings to adults, and decides which people you are compared with. We cannot check it, so we rely on what you tell us. If you choose under 13, the app stops and no account is created. If you choose 13 to 17, your data stays on your phone and no account is created.
  • Your Google user ID is turned into a scrambled ID using a secret key we hold. We store your progress under it.
  • Your progress copy holds your last 3,000 plays with the times, your settings and which challenges you finished.

Zest’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. If you join the rankings

Rankings are optional and for signed-in adults. When you join, we store:

  • The age group you chose, used to compare you only with people in the same group.
  • Your typical score in each game, the app version, and the times you joined and last played.

There is no leaderboard and no nickname. You only see a percentage for each game, such as “ahead of 60% of players in your age group”. Nobody sees your score, name, email or exact age, and you cannot see anyone else’s. A comparison appears only once at least 30 players are in your age group, and percentages are rounded to 5%. Your ranking entries are tied to your account and are deleted with it, or on their own with Delete my ranking entries.

5. Our service and who else sees data

Our service runs on Cloudflare. As with any web service, Cloudflare handles your IP address to deliver each request and keeps short-term request records. We do not store IP addresses in our own database. Reminders on iPhone are delivered by Apple’s push service (or Google’s or Mozilla’s, for other browsers). When you press the sign-in button, Google’s sign-in script loads and Google sees your IP address. The web version does not load any other outside fonts or trackers.

6. Ads (coming)

Zest is free. It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. A future version may show ads; there are none in the app yet. When there are, they will use:

  • Google AdMob (ads). AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and approximate location, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. This is collected by Google, not by us. See how Google uses advertising data. If you are under 18, or have not told us your age, we will show only non-personalised ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you will be asked for consent first, and you can change it later in Settings.

7. Who can use what

  • Zest is for players of every age, including children under 13. Zest asks for an age group (Under 13, 13 to 17 or 18 and over) after the first puzzle and does not ask for a date of birth.
  • Players under 13 see no ads. They cannot sign in or join rankings, so their plays and progress stay on their device.
  • Players aged 13 to 17 cannot sign in or join rankings either, and their data stays on their device. If ads are added, they will be non-personalised for players under 18.
  • For every age, the only things that can leave the device are the anonymous usage counts in section 2a, which carry no identifier and can be turned off in Settings, a Report a mistake note about a story (section 2), and, on iPhone, the reminder times described in section 2c if reminders are turned on.
  • Parents and guardians can ask us about a child’s data, or ask us to delete it, at orangepulplabs@gmail.com.
  • Signing in and rankings are for adults aged 18 and over, based on the age group you choose. We cannot check it.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

You are in control from Settings in the app:

  • Delete my ranking entries removes your scores from the comparison and turns rankings off.
  • Delete my cloud data removes your account, your progress copy and your ranking entries, and signs you out everywhere. Progress already on your phone stays there.
  • Uninstalling the app removes what is stored on the phone. On iPhone or in a browser, removing Zest from your Home Screen or clearing the website’s data removes what is stored there.
  • Turning reminders off in the iPhone Home Screen app deletes your push address and reminder times from our service.

You can also ask us to delete your data from this website without the app. We delete your data from our live database within 30 days of a request. Database backups can hold deleted data for up to 30 more days, and Cloudflare keeps short-term request records. Data nobody has used for 24 months is deleted automatically.

9. Your rights and how we use data

Depending on where you live, including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act and the GDPR, you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to the authority where you live. In India that is the Data Protection Board of India. Email us and we will help. Because we keep only a scrambled ID, we may ask you to sign in with the same Google account so we can find your data.

Our legal bases: your consent (sign-in, rankings, reminders on iPhone, ads), providing the service you asked for (progress sync), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and fair. Your data is processed in India and on Cloudflare and Google servers around the world, under their standard safeguards for international transfers.

10. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Ids are stored in scrambled form, and sign-in sessions end when you delete your account. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep sensitive information out of anything you share.

11. Zest is a game

Zest is a game for entertainment. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Your scores describe how you did in the games, nothing more.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the date above, tell you in the app, and ask again where the law requires it.
