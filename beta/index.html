OrangePulp Labs · Beta

Help test our apps before everyone else.

Before an app goes public, we run a closed test on Google Play with a small group. Testers get it early and decide what we fix first. Open now: Tidyup.

What you need

  • An Android phone.
  • A Google account (Gmail). Google Play only accepts Google accounts for tests.
  • About two weeks. Keep the app installed so the test counts.

How to join

  1. Email us your Google account address with the button below. It opens a ready-made message.
  2. We add you to the tester list on Google Play. This usually takes a day or two.
  3. We send you a link. Open it on your phone, tap Become a tester, then install the app from Google Play.

Email us to join the test →

What we do with your email

  • We use it only to add you to the Google Play tester list and to tell you about test builds.
  • We never sell or share it. Google holds the tester list, as part of how Play testing works.
  • If you say yes to future tests, we keep it to invite you to other OrangePulp Labs apps. Otherwise we delete it when the Tidyup test ends. Ask us to delete it any time.
