Test our apps before they launch.

We test each app with a small group on Google Play before it goes public. Right now we need testers for Zest, our story and logic puzzle game, and for Tidyup. You get the app early and tell us what is broken.

On an iPhone? You don’t need the beta: play Zest free on iPhone from your Home Screen.

What you need

  • An Android phone.
  • A Google account (Gmail). Google Play only accepts Google accounts for tests.
  • About two weeks. Keep the app installed so the test counts.

How to join

  1. Email us your Google account address. The button below opens a ready-made message.
  2. We add you to the tester list on Google Play. This usually takes a day or two.
  3. We send you a link. Open it on your phone, tap Become a tester, then install the app from Google Play.

Email us to join the test →

What we do with your email

  • We use it to add you to the Google Play tester list and to message you about test builds.
  • We do not sell it or share it. Google holds the tester list.
  • If you say yes to future tests, we keep it for other OrangePulp Labs apps. If not, we delete it when that app’s test ends. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
