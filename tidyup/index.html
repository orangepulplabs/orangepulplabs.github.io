Screenshots

Pick a size, then save or share. The app follows your phone's light or dark setting.

Tools

Compress photo

Shrink a photo to 20, 50, 100 or 200 KB, or type your own size.

Resize pixels

Exact width and height in px, cm or inches.

JPG to PDF

Put photos in one PDF, in the order you choose.

Scan document

Scan paper to a PDF with your camera.

Merge PDFs

Join several PDFs into one.

Split PDF

Take out the pages you need.

PDF to JPG

Save every page of a PDF as an image.

Privacy

Tidyup has no account and no server. Your photos and PDFs are processed on your phone and are never uploaded. Google’s ad services do collect some data. The privacy policy lists it.
