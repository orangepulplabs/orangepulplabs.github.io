The app

Three screens, no maze

Seven tools

One job each

No menus to dig through. Choose a tool, choose a file, get a result.

  1. 01
    Compress photoHit 20, 50, 100 or 200 KB, or type your own.
  2. 02
    JPG to PDFPhotos into one PDF, in the order you choose.
  3. 03
    Merge PDFsCombine several files into one.
  4. 04
    Split PDFPull out just the pages you need.
  5. 05
    PDF to JPGTurn every page into an image.
  6. 06
    Resize pixelsExact width and height in px, cm or inches.
  7. 07
    Scan documentCamera to PDF, with edge detection and cleanup.

Privacy

Your files stay yours

There is no account and no Tidyup server. Photos and PDFs are processed on your phone and never uploaded.

The only data that leaves your device is what Google’s ad and billing services collect. The privacy policy lists it in full.
