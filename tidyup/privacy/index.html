Tidyup privacy policy

Applies to the Tidyup Android app · Effective 10 November 2026 · Published by OrangePulp Labs

Tidyup works on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no Tidyup server. The photos and PDFs you open are never uploaded by us. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google’s ad services collect, explained below.

1. Your files stay on your phone

Tidyup compresses, resizes, converts, merges, splits and scans files entirely on your device. We do not receive, store or see your photos, PDFs or scans. Temporary working files are kept in the app's private storage and deleted automatically after about one day. Anything you choose to save goes to your gallery or Downloads folder, where you control it.

2. Data collected by third-party services

Tidyup is free and shows ads. To do that it uses these Google services:

  • Google AdMob (ads). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, operating system, app version), and approximate location, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. This is collected by Google, not by us. See how Google uses advertising data.
  • Google document scanner (Scan). The scan feature uses Google's on-device scanner through Google Play services. Scanning happens on your phone, and the camera permission is requested by Google's scanner when you use it.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent message. You can change your choice later from Privacy settings on the Tidyup home screen.

3. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob as described above.
  • Camera: only inside Google's scanner, only when you tap Scan.

Tidyup opens your files through Android's own file and photo pickers, so it can only see the files you select.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone ourselves. The only parties that receive data are the Google services listed in section 2.

5. Children

Tidyup is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age), and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads).
  • Opt out of personalized ads in your Google account's ad settings.
  • Uninstalling Tidyup removes everything it stored on your phone.

7. Retention and deletion

Because Tidyup keeps no account or server-side data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. Data held by Google (such as advertising data) is governed by Google's own policies.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

9. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: orangepulplabs@gmail.com
